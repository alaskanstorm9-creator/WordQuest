# WordQuest — Master Direction

## Core pillars
WORDS → HEROES → STORY → WORLD BOSS → ARENA → SOCIAL

## Economy
- Gems: summons/premium currency.
- Coins: normal hero upgrades.
- Hero XP: hero leveling.
- Duplicate copies: Ascension.
- Single summon: 100 Gems.
- Ten summon: 1,000 Gems.
- Chapter completion: 250 Gems.

## Story
20 chapters × 15 stages = 300 stages. Story consumes Energy. Bosses use word-length resistances/weaknesses, disabled letters, board disruption, and combined mechanics in later chapters.

## World Boss
Always free; no Energy. Six bosses rotate Monday–Saturday. Sunday unlocks all six. Each boss has unique mechanics. Daily rank awards scale with placement and unlock cosmetic titles.

## Arena
60-second synchronous duel. Both players receive the same letter board and neutral boss/modifier. Highest single-match damage wins. Ranked mode normalizes raw progression while preserving strategic hero abilities. Friendly duels are unranked/free.

## Heroes
Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Ultra. Hero abilities modify word strategy rather than replacing word skill. Duplicate heroes advance Ascension and improve/evolve abilities. Maxed duplicates convert to a future universal resource.

## Social
Friends, friend challenges, player profiles, daily friend gifts, server/world chat, Arena chat, World Boss chat, blocking/reporting and moderation.

## Supporting systems
Player level, saved loadouts, Daily Quests, achievements, login calendar, inbox, Codex, Bestiary/Boss Intel, summon pity/odds, settings/accessibility, seasons/events, dictionary rules, server-side validation and anti-cheat.

## v0.1
Playable local prototype proving Story combat, damage, heroes, currencies, Energy, rewards, summons, duplicates, Codex and Bestiary before online/backend systems.
