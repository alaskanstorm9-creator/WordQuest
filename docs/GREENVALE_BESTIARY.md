# Chapter 1 Bestiary — Greenvale

1. **Moss Goblin** — A harmless root-cave scavenger until the Rootstone disturbance drove its colony above ground. Black rune-scars suggest exposure to corrupted Lifeword.
2. **Forest Boar** — An enormous old boar maddened by the forest's draining vitality. Its tusks have begun growing rune-like ridges.
3. **Briar Witch** — Not a human witch, but a sentient knot of ancient thorn-vines that imitates a cloaked figure. It awakened when the Rootstone reversed.
4. **Cave Ogre** — A hulking relic of an older age released when forgotten stone doors opened beneath the hills.
5. **Stonefang** — Mini-boss. A rune-chained predator deliberately released from an outer Rootstone prison. The broken lock bears the Hollow Scribe's mark.
6. **Bog Troll** — A regenerative marsh creature displaced when poisoned Lifeword seeped into Greenvale's wetlands.
7. **Rune Knight** — An empty suit of Anchor-warden armor animated by corrupted commands. Its original inscription reads: PROTECT THE ROOT.
8. **Hex Crow** — A giant carrion bird feeding on loose fragments of corrupted language. Its cry temporarily erases a letter from nearby runes.
9. **Hill Giant** — A confused ancient giant awakened far from the age it remembers. Its rampage is driven by fear as much as aggression.
10. **Iron Troll** — Elite boss. Once an artificial guardian of the Rootstone's outer mechanism. The Hollow Scribe rewrote its command-runes from GUARD to BREAK.
11. **Wyrmling** — A young subterranean drake drawn upward by concentrated Lifeword leaking through the damaged Anchor.
12. **Grave Mage** — The animated remains of an ancient Anchor scholar. Corruption forces it to repeat the final defensive spell it cast centuries ago.
13. **Mountain Drake** — A mature drake that followed the Wyrmling's trail and now feeds directly on exposed Lifeword.
14. **Ancient Golem** — The final internal guardian. It attacks because the heroes are not recognized by the vanished civilization that built the Anchor.
15. **Gravemaw** — Chapter boss. An enormous deep-earth beast imprisoned beside the Rootstone as both prisoner and living regulator. After centuries of confinement, the reversed Anchor floods it with unstable Lifeword. Defeating Gravemaw allows the heroes to reach and sever the Hollow Scribe's conduit.

## Art progression
Stages 1–4: recognizable woodland fantasy creatures with subtle corruption.
Stage 5: first obvious ancient prison imagery—broken rune chains.
Stages 6–9: stronger black/violet corruption and drained environments.
Stage 10: ancient Anchor technology becomes visually explicit.
Stages 11–14: underground rune machinery, floating fragments and reversed writing.
Stage 15: Gravemaw combines natural stone/earth anatomy, broken ancient restraints and bright corrupted Lifeword fissures.

Boss silhouettes should remain readable at phone size. Avoid overloading early enemies with effects so visual escalation remains meaningful.
