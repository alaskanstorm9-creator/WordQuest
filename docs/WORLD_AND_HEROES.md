# WordQuest — Mythology & Hero Roster

The world sits at the collision point of two mythic cosmologies. The roots of Yggdrasil have crossed the hidden roads of Olympus after the World-Rune shattered. The Norns and the Greek Fates discover that they have been recording the same destiny in different alphabets. Monsters, lost souls, relics and heroes now cross between realms.

The central mystery is not "Norse versus Greek." Someone is deliberately breaking the language that keeps the realms separate. Pip Shadowstep steals the first rune-key, Lyra Dawnkeeper hears the shared prophecy, Mira Moonscribe discovers the crossroads, and Aurelia Sunblade follows the trail of the stolen sacred flame. Their stories become the player's entry into the larger conflict.

The launch roster contains exactly 40 heroes: 10 Mages, 10 Rogues, 10 Warriors and 10 Clerics. It also contains exactly 10 Common, 10 Uncommon, 10 Rare and 10 Ultra heroes. Each character is inspired by mythic themes while remaining a WordQuest character with an original role in the setting.

Class identity:
- Mage — time, rune manipulation, letter transformation.
- Rogue — bonus tiles, precision, high-value board positions.
- Warrior — dependable direct damage and forceful strikes.
- Cleric — rerolls, recovery and reward utility.

Hero actives will later charge through valid words made. Class synergy, passive traits and charged active abilities are separate systems. Story-only time passives do not apply to Arena or World Boss.
