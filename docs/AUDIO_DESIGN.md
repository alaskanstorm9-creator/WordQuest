# WordQuest Audio Direction

## Musical identity
WordQuest should sound like two mythic worlds colliding: Nordic folk color (tagelharpa-style bowed strings, low frame drums, horns, drones) meeting Greek/Mediterranean color (lyre-like plucks, hand percussion, reed/flute colors). The score should be adventurous rather than grim so long play sessions remain comfortable.

## Core music cues
- Title / Main Theme — memorable 60–90 second theme built around a five-note WordQuest motif; lyre begins it, low Nordic strings answer it, then drums and horns unite both.
- Greenvale — warm plucked strings, flute, light hand drum, forest ambience.
- Normal Battle — faster version of the main motif, restrained percussion so word finding stays mentally clear.
- Boss Battle — heavier drums and horn pulses; add layers at 50% and 25% boss HP.
- Victory — 3–4 second heroic motif using the same five notes.
- Summon — quiet rune hum -> rising chime -> rarity-dependent reveal hit.
- Heroes — calm harp/lyre and bowed-string arrangement.
- Store — lively crossroads-market arrangement, hand percussion and plucked strings.
- World Boss — massive drums/choir texture, but no distracting melodic density during active word play.
- Arena — tight rhythmic pulse designed around the one-minute match clock.

## SFX language
- Tile tap: short carved-wood click.
- Tile deselect: softer reverse click.
- Valid Strike: sword/rune impact layered with a low magical thump.
- Invalid word: muted stone knock; never harsh.
- Letter Streak: rising rune chime for streak 2–5.
- Rogue x2 tile: quick metallic coin/blade sparkle.
- Mage time bonus: glassy clock/rune shimmer.
- Warrior synergy: shield-and-sword accent after the normal hit.
- Cleric free reroll: warm bell/chime.
- New Letters: parchment/stone shuffle with rune sweep.
- Boss hit: creature-specific impact plus restrained screen-shake sound.
- Boss defeat: heavy impact, short silence, then victory motif.
- Energy gain: lightning bottle crackle.
- Coin reward: 2–3 coin clinks.
- Gem reward: crystalline ping.
- Summon rarity: Common wood/bronze, Uncommon silver chime, Rare orchestral hit, Ultra layered choir + thunder + crystal.
- Ability ready: distinct two-note cue; ability activation gets a class-specific sound.

## Class sonic signatures
Mage: glass, runes, reversed bells, airy shimmer.
Rogue: leather, knife swishes, quick metallic ticks.
Warrior: steel, shields, drums, low impacts.
Cleric: bells, choir breath, warm resonant chimes.

## Implementation notes
Music and SFX need separate volume controls plus mute. Avoid continuous vocals during timed word play. All important gameplay feedback must remain readable visually so audio is never required. Production assets should be original or properly licensed.
